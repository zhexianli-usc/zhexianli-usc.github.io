## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy centers on the belief that effective learning occurs when students can connect abstract theoretical concepts to tangible, real-world applications. I strive to create an inclusive and engaging classroom environment where complex mathematical ideas in control theory, optimization, and systems engineering become accessible through practical examples and hands-on experiences.

## Educational Innovation

I am committed to incorporating modern pedagogical approaches:
- **Active Learning**: Using interactive problem-solving and group discussions
- **Technology Integration**: Leveraging computational tools and simulation software
- **Real-World Applications**: Connecting theory to infrastructure and transportation systems
- **Inclusive Teaching**: Adapting methods to support diverse learning styles and backgrounds

I believe in the importance of mentoring students not just in technical skills, but also in developing critical thinking, research methodology, and professional communication abilities that will serve them throughout their careers.
